# Setup guide

How to connect the pump and the robot arm to the computer before starting
RainMixer.

## Robot arm

The order matters: the microchip (Arduino Nano) must be connected over USB
before the 12 V power supply is plugged in.

**Connecting**

1. Connect the microchip to the computer with its USB cable.
2. Then plug in the 12 V power supply (PSU).

**Disconnecting** — the same steps in reverse order:

1. Unplug the 12 V power supply first.
2. Then unplug the USB cable from the computer.

## Pump

Connect the pump to the computer with a USB-A to Mini-USB cable. Nothing else
is needed.
